# App mockup — habit-tracker iOS app

**Command:** `/appmockup`

## What it does

A realistic phone frame with a plausible UI, not a screenshot of iOS Settings.

## Works best for

SaaS, consumer apps, onboarding. Specify OS and one screen.

## Quick prompt

```text
/appmockup habit tracker iOS
```

`/appmockup` is shorthand. If the model ignores the slash token, use the expanded prompt.

## Better prompt

```text
Create a photoreal iPhone mockup showing one screen of a habit-tracker app.

UI: today's habits, streak count, simple checkboxes, calm typography.
Device at a slight angle on a desk, soft daylight. 16:9.
Readable UI, not lorem-ipsum blur. No Apple marketing lockup.
```
