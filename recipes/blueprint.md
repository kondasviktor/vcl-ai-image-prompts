# Blueprint — Tesla Cybertruck

**Command:** `/blueprint`

## What it does

Turns the subject into an orthographic technical blueprint: white lines, annotations, blue grid background.

## Works best for

Vehicles, machines, architecture, furniture, consumer electronics.

## Quick prompt

```text
/blueprint Tesla Cybertruck
orthographic technical drawing
front, side and top views
white technical annotations
blue blueprint background
16:9
```

`/blueprint` is shorthand. If the model ignores the slash token, use the expanded prompt.

## Better prompt

```text
Create a detailed technical blueprint of a Tesla Cybertruck.

Show:
- front, side, and top orthographic views on one sheet
- white construction lines and dimensions
- callouts for body panels, wheels, and light bars
- blue blueprint grid background
- no photorealistic materials, no environment, no people

Clean engineering drawing. High contrast white on blue. 16:9. No extra decorative text.
```
