# Timeline — history of the iPhone

**Command:** `/timeline`

## What it does

A dated sequence left-to-right or top-to-bottom, not a collage of random phones.

## Works best for

Product history, company history, protocol evolution.

## Quick prompt

```text
/timeline history of the iPhone
```

`/timeline` is shorthand. If the model ignores the slash token, use the expanded prompt.

## Better prompt

```text
Create a horizontal timeline infographic of the iPhone from 2007 to today.

Evenly spaced nodes, year labels, one device silhouette per generation (not every SKU).
Minimal text. Neutral background. 16:9.
Not a photoreal table of 40 phones. Not a YouTube thumbnail.
```
